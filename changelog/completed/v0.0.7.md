# Dot Tank Arena v0.0.7 — Unified World Revision

Status: implemented and verified

## Delivered

- Replaced five arenas with one persistent 6000×3800 shared world, approximately three times the previous playable area.
- Increased default capacity to 30 humans and 12 utility-based bots to maintain activity across the larger world.
- Added connected Sandbox, Bush Maze, Garden, Driveway, and Toy Fort regions to the authoritative snapshot.
- Replaced the old stacked terrain composition with one regional tile renderer using 32×16 2:1 tiles.
- Added region landmarks to the minimap.
- Added full bush concealment for enemy tanks, names, and levels, with reveal windows after firing, dashing, or taking damage.
- Added assists and assist XP through recent damage contribution tracking.
- Added elimination recovery equal to 50% of the eliminator's missing health.
- Strengthened dash acceleration, knockback, impact damage, and momentum transfer while retaining 0.25/0.4/0.6-second stagger tiers.
- Extended ring-out attribution to recent attackers.
- Slowed bot reactions and turning, increased aim spread, and reduced firing frequency.
- Restricted tank colors to high-contrast red/pink, cyan, white, orange, purple, and blue values.
- Preserved authoritative movement, shooting, collision, dots, leveling, objectives, power-ups, reconnect grace, desktop controls, and mobile controls.

## Verification

- TypeScript production build passed.
- Multiplayer smoke test validates one world, five regions, movement, dash state, bots, and synchronized snapshots.
