# Dot Tank Arena Master Prompt v0.0.7

Unified World Edition — core vision locked and coherent.

## Project vision

Build a multiplayer pixel-art browser game about toy tanks fighting across one giant persistent outdoor battlefield. The world should support exploration, many small fights, environmental play, objectives, and natural conflict hotspots. Its world flow should feel closer to Mope.io than to a round-based arena shooter.

The game is not a military simulator, an empty arena shooter, a round-based game, a 2.5D game, or a height/elevation game.

## Unified world

- Replace separate maps with one shared battlefield approximately three times the current area.
- Remove loading, map switching, voting, and transitions.
- Connect recognizable Sandbox, Bush, Garden, Driveway, and Toy Fort regions naturally.
- Fill populated worlds first if capacity ever requires more than one world.

## Pixel-art direction

- Everything must use sprites, tiles, pixel terrain, and pixel effects.
- Remove circles, gradients, smooth/vector-looking gameplay assets, and mixed visual styles.
- Use 2:1 pixel construction where appropriate while keeping gameplay fully 2D.
- Communicate plastic toy tanks outdoors through bricks, sand structures, rocks, sticks, flowers, leaves, and chalk.
- Tank colors must contrast terrain; prefer red, blue, purple, pink, orange, cyan, and white with strong outlines.

## Concealment and readability

- Bushes fully conceal enemy tanks, names, and levels.
- Concealment ends temporarily when firing, dashing, or taking damage, and ends when leaving the bush.

## Combat and recovery

- Keep damage, hit detection, kills, and assists server-authoritative.
- Strengthen dash knockback, impact damage, and momentum transfer.
- High-force dash hits stagger for roughly 0.25, 0.4, or 0.6 seconds; stagger prevents moving, turning, firing, and dashing.
- Progressive boundary damage creates ring-outs, with elimination credit for recent displacement.
- An eliminator restores 50% of their missing health.
- Every five dots gives a small heal.

## Progression and AI

- Award XP for dots, damage, eliminations, assists, and objectives.
- Keep level upgrades small and capped across health, speed, fire rate, and dash power.
- Use utility-based Hunter, Collector, Berserker, Sniper, Opportunist, and Survivor bots.
- Bots need reaction delays, turn acceleration, imperfect tracking, aim spread, misses, and reduced firing frequency.

## Objectives and navigation

- Spawn a sandbox sandcastle event every five minutes.
- Destroying it creates a 90-second temporary buff: Reinforced Plastic, Fresh Batteries, Turbo Treads, Heavy Bumper, or Shock Absorbers.
- The minimap displays player position, objective, boundary, and regional landmarks.

## Development rules

Inspect first, preserve existing work and Git history, build before committing, pull/rebase before pushing, maintain authoritative networking, prioritize interaction/readability, keep gameplay 2D, and preserve the outdoor toy-tank identity.
